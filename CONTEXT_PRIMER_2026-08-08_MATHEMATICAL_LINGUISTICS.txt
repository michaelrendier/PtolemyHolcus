3. THE MISSING EQUATION IS A TYPE SYSTEM — PREGROUP GRAMMAR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the run-up. Lambek 1999, "Type Grammar Revisited". A pregroup is a
partially ordered monoid in which every element has a LEFT and a RIGHT adjoint.
Adjacent adjoints cancel:

    n · nʳ → 1          nˡ · n → 1

    noun                     n
    adjective                n · nˡ
    intransitive verb        nʳ · s
    transitive verb          nʳ · s · nˡ

    "John likes Mary":   n · (nʳ · s · nˡ) · n  →  s

A SENTENCE IS GRAMMATICAL IFF ITS TYPE STRING REDUCES TO s.

That is exactly Cody's "line the words up according to their definitions and
the connecting words to tie them together", formalised in 1999, and it is an
ALGEBRA — implementable in an afternoon.

His content/pathway split is already inside it: content words carry n and s;
connecting words are the ones whose types are almost entirely ADJOINTS — pure
cancellation machinery with no content of their own. That is
"connections, not sections", written down.

9. STANDING WAVES / PROPAGATION → BOUND vs SCATTERING STATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
He asked: is it useful to think of content words as STANDING WAVES and
connecting words as WAVEFORM PROPAGATION? YES, with one inversion to fix.

    content word    a MODE       localized, fixed structure, resonates in place
    pathway word    TRANSPORT    carries no content, moves between modes

THE INVERSION: standing waves are the QUANTIZED ones (discrete spectrum from
boundary conditions); travelling waves have continuous spectrum. But content is
the OPEN class and pathway the CLOSED class — the quantization lands on the
wrong side.

THE FIX, and it uses the box-kite result: content words are modes of MANY
DIFFERENT CAVITIES — quantized within each concept's bounded region, but
unboundedly many regions ⟹ open class. Pathway words are transitions BETWEEN
cavities, and the routes between are topologically limited (they go through
e₀, the only place the charts touch) ⟹ closed class, few, quantized by
TOPOLOGY rather than by boundary conditions. Both discrete, different reasons,
counts on the right sides.

THE SHARPER FRAME (standard Hilbert-space decomposition, not analogy):
    BOUND states       discrete spectrum, normalizable, localized  ← content
    SCATTERING states  continuous spectrum, extended, carry flux   ← pathway
  ⟹ A SENTENCE IS A SCATTERING EVENT. Bound states in, interaction through
    transport, bound states out — and what you compute is the S-MATRIX: what
    went in, what came out, AND WITH WHAT PHASE. The phase is the meaning,
    which is the holonomy point again. A TAUTOLOGY IS THE IDENTITY S-MATRIX.
  His "resonance or dissonance" reads directly: a grammatical sentence is
  where amplitudes ADD (pregroup types cancel and reduce to s); ungrammatical
  is where they do not. TYPE REDUCTION AS AN INTERFERENCE CONDITION.
