PART 1 — CONTEXT BOX-KITES (status: DONE, closed, foundation for everything else)
========================================================================

--- 1.1 wordnet_boxkite.py (VAPMIP/wordnet_boxkite.py) ---

`context_vector(synset)` — 19 real WordNet relation methods (hypernyms,
hyponyms, member/substance/part holonyms+meronyms, attributes,
entailments, causes, also_sees, verb_groups, similar_tos, topic/region/
usage_domains), each entry = `compress_count(len(relation_targets))` =
`round(log2(n+1))`. `context_code(synset)` = product of `CONTEXT_PRIMES[i]
** vector[i]` — a genuine multiplicative prime-hash address, PW3
(`spiral_is_additive`) applied: `address(p*q) = address(p) + address(q)`.

CLOSED on a real positive result (Phase 31 wiki,
docs/wiki/Tuning-the-Engine/31_wordnet_box_kites_and_the_pile_concept_emergence.md):
an abstract "pile of something" concept (bank.n.01/03/05's shared relation
shape) detected via context-vector collision, 4/15 exact matches vs a
5.5% random-control base rate (p~=0.01).

--- 1.2 sentence_context.py (VAPMIP/sentence_context.py) ---

`build_sentence_context(text)` -> `SentenceBoxKite(leaves, skipped,
root_vector, root_code)`. `root_vector` = componentwise SUM of each
resolvable word's `context_vector` — NOT a new hash layer, explicitly
confirmed as PW3 reapplied (Cody's own correction mid-build: "the combined
context doesn't necessarily need additional prime hashing"). WSD is a
STUB: first WordNet sense only (`wn.synsets(word)[0]`), a real, named,
not-yet-fixed limitation.

`neighborhood_corpus(leaves, max_per_relation=5, exclude_input=True)` —
candidate pool for RESPONSE construction: every synset directly related
(any of the 19 methods) to an input leaf.

`nearest_synsets(target_vector, corpus, top_k=5)` — ranks candidates by
plain L1 distance (`sum(abs(a-b))`) between target_vector and each
candidate's own `context_vector`. THIS IS THE PIECE PART 4 IS ABOUT —
it does NOT use any Scale-engine fold machinery, just raw Manhattan
distance on compress_count-scaled integers.

--- 1.3 monad_grammar.bin (built this session) ---

`VAPMIP/tools/make_grammar_bin.py` -> `VAPMIP/PtolC/monad_grammar.bin`
(gitignored, real, 104,796 bytes). 5,952 irregular exceptions
(`/usr/share/wordnet/{noun,verb,adj,adv}.exc`, WordNet's own data) + 25
regular suffix rules (`nltk`'s `MORPHOLOGICAL_SUBSTITUTIONS`). Two-tier
hierarchy: exceptions checked first (closed, memorized), rules second
(open, generative) — the same hierarchy `morphy()` already runs silently
inside every `wn.synsets()` call. Round-trip verified exact.

--- 1.4 monad_phonetic.bin (built this session) ---

`VAPMIP/tools/make_phonetic_bin.py` -> `VAPMIP/PtolC/monad_phonetic.bin`
(gitignored, real, 3,715,616 bytes). 123,455 words from NLTK's `cmudict`
(CMU Pronouncing Dictionary — General American pronunciation specifically,
NOT British/other varieties, a real honest scope limit). ARPAbet phonemes
carry real stress digits (0/1/2); multiple pronunciations kept per word
(heteronyms: `record`/`record`, `present`/`present`, genuinely different
stress = genuinely different meaning/POS). `thaumaturge` is NOT covered —
a real, honest coverage gap (cmudict is ~123k common words, not
exhaustive).

`syllable_count(pron)` / `primary_stress_index(pron)` are DERIVED from the
stress digits, not separately stored — same "don't store what you can
recompute" discipline as `compress_count`.

PART 2 — THE SCALE ENGINE (built this session, ValaQuenta + SFR)
========================================================================

Full engine protocol: `ValaQuenta/modules/scale/` (maths.py/tools.py, 10
equations) + `SedenionFactoralRelativity/engine/lineage.py` PW13-15 (same
results, independent port) + wiki pages in both repos + Ainulindale
wiki/95. Core functions actually used by the constructor:

`mobius_fold(Z, Z0)` = `Gamma = (Z-Z0)/(Z+Z0)` — the Smith-chart fold.
`scale_factor(Z, Z0)` = `|dGamma/dZ|` — PROVEN exact power law far from
anchor, `scale_factor ~ 2|Z0|/r^2`, confirmed by direct computation
converging to exactly 2.0 as r->infinity (Z0=1).

Also this session: PW16 `unit_lineage_decompose` (units as a geometry, no
numeric content, EQUATION_INDEX narrowing a dimension signature to
candidate physical laws — the literal analogue of context_vector
narrowing a word to candidate synsets). `SedenionFactoralRelativity`
engine now 44/44.

Real, load-bearing finding from testing five candidate ring-pairs: `J_red`
+ `J_blue` = 1.0000000000 EXACTLY (checked to 10 decimals) — degenerate,
one number wearing two labels, NOT two independent rings, because
`cam_encode` returns a unit-normalized vector split into non-overlapping
halves. Caught, not hidden. `σ_RB[k] = ψ[k]·ψ[k^4]` was later shown to
equal `(r_k^2/2)·sin(2*theta_k)` EXACTLY (verified to 3.6e-15) where
`(r_k,theta_k)` is the polar decomposition of the XOR-4-paired components
— meaning σ_RB is scale^2 times a pure angular term, not scale-invariant
itself.

PART 3 — constructor.py (VAPMIP/constructor.py) — THE TEST FLIGHT
========================================================================

Built and pushed this session (commit c16cbf8). Two ORTHOGONAL constraints
folded together to rank real candidate words:

  ring1 (semantic axis) = L1 distance from a sentence's root_vector to a
    candidate's context_vector (nearest_synsets' own distance metric)
  ring2 (scale axis) = |candidate_syllable_count - target_syllables|
    (monad_phonetic.bin)

Folded via `mobius_fold(Z, Z0)` after MIN-MAX NORMALISING both axes to
[0,1] within each query's own retrieved candidate pool (NOT a fixed
global anchor — that was bug #1, see below), then ranked by `|Gamma|`
ascending.

--- 3.1 Two real bugs caught and fixed in the same pass, not hidden ---

BUG 1: raw semantic_dist (typically 11-17, a sum across 19
compress_count dimensions) completely swamped raw syllable-gap (typically
0-3) against a fixed, hand-picked `Z0=(3.0,1.5)` — two different
`target_syllables` values produced the IDENTICAL word ranking. Caught by
literally comparing two runs side by side, not assumed correct. FIXED by
min-max normalising both axes to [0,1] within the pool actually retrieved
for that specific query, then folding against a fixed `Z0=(0.5,0.5)` (the
centre of the normalised square).

BUG 2 (found immediately after fixing #1): for a NARROW candidate pool
(short/common seed words -> mostly 1-2 syllable candidates), normalising
across only 2 distinct raw values collapses many candidates to identical
`|Gamma|` scores. Which TIED word prints first then depends on Python's
hash-randomized set iteration order between process runs, NOT on the
actual target — confirmed by running the identical query twice and
getting different top-8 words both times. RESOLVED (not eliminated as a
class) by widening retrieval (`pool_cap=10`, deeper `neighborhood_corpus`)
so more syllable diversity survives — confirmed the ranking then
genuinely reorders between `target_syllables=1.0` and `=4.0`
("interpret"/"edifice" vs "building" swapping rank #1).

--- 3.2 Honest current scope ---

WORD SELECTION ONLY. Does NOT yet:
  - inflect selected words via monad_grammar.bin
  - order words into a real dependency structure (a pathway_decomposition-
    shaped DAG was proposed, not built)
  - apply the punctuation/tone construction layer (designed conceptually
    this session — the "conversation? Confuse(ed)! Then Speak..."
    re-punctuation example, invisible Unicode tonal markers via the
    U+E0000-E007F Tag block or variation selectors, whitespace-type
    semantics — NONE of this has any code yet)
  - handle any language other than English (WordNet + cmudict +
    MORPHOLOGICAL_SUBSTITUTIONS are all English-specific; explicit TODO
    in VAPMIP/TODO.md, Cody: "we have got to more rigorously test the
    inter-language stuff...(not now)")

`Z0=(0.5,0.5)` (post-normalisation) is a reasonable, symmetric choice but
NOT independently validated against real usage data the way e.g. the
Flesch-Kincaid formula itself is.

PART 4 — SEMANTIC PRIME HASH RETOOLING (named as a next step, NOT started)
========================================================================

Cody, verbatim, mid-session: *"catching the semantic prime hash up to the
maths won't be hard once we have the constructor designed."* Now that the
constructor exists (Part 3), this is unblocked but genuinely not begun.

The precise gap: `nearest_synsets()` (Part 1.2) compares two
`context_vector`s with plain L1 distance — it has NEVER been routed
through any of the Scale engine's own machinery (`mobius_fold`,
`scale_factor`, `cross_ratio`). The semantic/prime-hash system and the
Scale engine were built in completely separate parts of this session and
have not been unified.

What "retooling" concretely would mean, to be designed next session, not
assumed here:
  - Does treating two context_vectors' comparison AS a two-ring Smith-
    chart fold (rather than raw L1 distance) change anything real? This
    needs to be TESTED the way every other candidate ring-pair this
    session was tested (real data, check for degeneracy, check the
    |Gamma| spread isn't saturated/uniform) — not assumed to be an
    improvement just because it reuses more of the engine.
  - `context_code` (the multiplicative prime-hash address itself,
    `context_vector`'s product-of-primes-to-powers form) has a natural
    LOG structure (`log(context_code) = sum(vector[i] * log(prime[i]))`)
    that has never been connected to the Scale engine's own "folding IS
    log-then-bound" master identity (`Gamma = tanh(half*ln(Z/Z0))`,
    verified exact this session). This is a real, unexplored, possibly
    load-bearing connection — the semantic hash may ALREADY be halfway
    to Scale-engine-native without anyone having noticed, or it may not
    connect cleanly at all. Genuinely open, not pre-decided.
  - Whatever the outcome, apply the SAME degeneracy discipline that
    caught J_red/J_blue and the constructor's two bugs before trusting
    any specific retooling as real progress.
