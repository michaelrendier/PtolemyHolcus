15. LOCUS vs FOCUS, AND CATASTROPHE THEORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOCUS = the SET of points satisfying a condition. FOCUS = a distinguished
POINT. Foci are INPUTS to the condition; the locus is the resulting SET.
A locus is NOT a basin of foci (that would be a basin of attraction, which is
dynamical systems, not geometry).

In Cody's usage the relation is: focus = ONE ZD (one origin); locus = the SET
of all 42 available origins.

A FOCUS IS A CAUSTIC COLLAPSED TO A POINT. A caustic is what a focus becomes
once you stop idealizing it — the point-focus is non-generic (structurally
unstable), the caustic is generic. So "the locus is the negative space of the
focus" is right, and the mechanism is: the locus is what the focus becomes
under perturbation.
