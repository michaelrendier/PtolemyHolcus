FINAL, CONFIRMED (Cody, 2026-08-25): raw counts used directly as exponents
made context_code(tree.n.01) a 364-digit number (hyponyms=180 -> 83^180).

log-compressed BEFORE becoming an exponent (not "the exponent gets
logged", which would be backwards/nonsensical since log and exponentiation
are inverses -- named compress_count() specifically to avoid that
confusion). tree.n.01 -> 28 digits with this. KEPT as the default
context_vector() going forward, per Cody: "perfect...keep the log
compressed exponents...that should be more than enough room for the
english language to breathe and also coagulate around context." Also
partially helps the near-miss problem (fixes the worst heavy-tail
outliers, e.g. a hyponyms-180-vs-181 case) but does NOT fix small-count
near-misses (0 vs 1 is barely compressed by log2) -- the vector-distance
tool above is still the real fix for "nearly the same", still open.

raw_context_vector() kept in the file for reference/debugging (uncompressed).

--- 1.5e RESOLVED (2026-08-25): direction past the fixed point is rotation-
         order, not ordinal magnitude -- "order of arrival is how you draw
         a circle" ---

Grew out of testing rotary_rerun_boxkite_monad.py (see 2.1) and asking what
"direction" even means once data has left the real line. Chain, precise:

  ℝ is an ordered field; ℂ provably is not (any field with a root of -1
  can't be ordered -- squares are >=0 in an ordered field, i^2=-1 isn't).
  So ">"/"<" -- the whole basis of "up/down" -- does not exist past the
  fixed point (sigma=1/2). CORRECTED once more precisely by Cody:
  magnitude |z| DOES still exist and IS ordinal (a real, comparable
  quantity) -- but that's the RADIUS of the polar form. What the
  imaginary part actually carries is the ANGLE theta -- a rotation, not
  a quantity to rank. "i values are rotations, not quantizations."

  So "direction" past the fixed point can only be ORDER OF ARRIVAL --
  which operation (Add/Scale/Sign) fired, in what sequence -- not a
  linear ordinal position. And this is not a fresh metaphor grafted on:
  it is ALREADY the operative mechanism in two places on record --
  rotary_rerun.c's sigma_self (real ratio, ordinal, "sits") vs the
  Noether current (Im(z*dz), entirely phase, "it points") is the same
  cut; and wiki Phase 3's port geometry (PORT_STEP=pi/3, rotor advances
  EXACTLY one port-step per call, port_idx = round(theta/PORT_STEP) % 6)
